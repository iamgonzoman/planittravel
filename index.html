"What should I know about the Fez medina before I arrive?"
The Fez medina is the world's largest car-free urban area — zero vehicles, 9,000 alleys. A few things that will actually help you:

🧭 Get lost on purpose. The medina cannot be navigated by GPS — download offline maps before you go.

💬 Learn three Darija phrases. "Bshal hade?" (How much?), "Ghali bzzaf!" (Too expensive!), "Wakha ana mshy" (I'm leaving). That last one drops prices instantly.

💳 Cash only in the medina. Carry MAD. Exchange at Wafa Bank — 8% better rate than the airport.
