What it does

Your AI travel companion.
For every explorer.

One app that plans your trip, speaks any language, guides you while you walk, and works when there is no signal.

☀️
Sol — Your AI Companion
Ask Sol anything about your trip. What to pack for Tanzania in July. Best restaurants in Fez. How to negotiate in a Moroccan market. Sol knows your destination, your dates, and your budget.
✈️
Instant Trip Planning
Paste any booking confirmation — Airbnb, hotels, flights, Uber. Sol reads it and builds your complete day-by-day itinerary instantly. Works with every booking service on earth.
🎙️
Live Voice Interpreter
Hold to speak, release to translate. Arabic, Farsi, Swahili, French, Spanish dialects — spoken back in the local language with phonetics shown on screen.
💬
Conversation Mode
Two people, one phone, any language. You speak English, they hear Arabic. They speak Darija, you hear English. Live two-way interpreter — no human translator needed.
📷
Camera Translation
Point your camera at any sign, menu, or street name. PlanitTravel reads it and translates it instantly. Works in Arabic, Chinese, Thai, Russian — any script.
📡
Works Fully Offline
Download before you go. Your full itinerary, maps, and 200+ phrases in your target language all work with zero internet. No signal in the Serengeti? Still works.
